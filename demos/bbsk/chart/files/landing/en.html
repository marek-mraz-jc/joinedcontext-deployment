An AI assistant on your region's data: try it

Ask about cycle routes, bridges, schools, pharmacies or road accidents in the Banská Bystrica Region, in Slovak or English. The assistant answers from www.bbsk.sk and from the open data of the region and the city of Banská Bystrica, and names the source of every fact.

A demo by joinedcontext for the Banská Bystrica self-governing region. It is not an official BBSK service.

Try it

Choose a question: the assistant opens and offers the same question among its suggestions. Or write your own.

How it works

  1. The region's and the city's data

    The open data of BBSK (opendata.bbsk.sk) and of the city of Banská Bystrica are loaded into an NGSI-LD context space, in the standard Smart Data Models.

  2. Endpoint and rules

    Every public dataset has its Endpoint with access rules. The public can only read; nobody changes anything through it.

  3. MCP

    The assistant asks the Endpoints through the open MCP protocol, as any AI assistant you connect yourself does.

  4. The region's website (RAG)

    The assistant searches www.bbsk.sk and www.banskabystrica.sk in an index of its own, refreshed every week.

The demo holds only the data of BBSK and the city of Banská Bystrica. Every dataset is in the catalogue: the Banská Bystrica self-governing region and the city of Banská Bystrica.

Connect your own AI assistant (MCP)

Your own AI assistant gets the same data and the same website. The connector only reads and needs no login and no key.

Connector addresshttps://api.__DOMAIN__/api/mcp/d/bbsk-demo

Claude (claude.ai and Claude Desktop)

  1. Open Customize, then Connectors, choose + Add and Add custom connector.
  2. Name it, for example, “BBSK demo” and paste the address above.
  3. Leave the advanced settings empty and choose Add.

Then ask: “Where is a map of the region's cycle routes?”

Claude Code, VS Code and other MCP clients

claude mcp add --transport http bbsk-demo https://api.__DOMAIN__/api/mcp/d/bbsk-demo
{ "servers": { "bbsk-demo": { "type": "http", "url": "https://api.__DOMAIN__/api/mcp/d/bbsk-demo" } } }

ChatGPT: follow OpenAI's guide to connecting a custom connector.

Privacy and limits

Answers are written by AI

They may contain mistakes. Check important facts in the linked sources or at www.bbsk.sk/kontakty.

What we keep

Conversations are kept for 90 days to improve the service, with personal data masked. Do not enter personal data. This page uses no cookies and no analytics. The assistant has a daily limit; when it is spent, it says so and answers again the next day.
